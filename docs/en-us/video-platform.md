# Publishing platform list

::: tip What this page is for
This is the **exact scope** of the two checkboxes in the *Fengxing Mizong* (《风行迷踪》) Recording Participation and Authorization Confirmation: "Platforms in mainland China" and "Platforms outside mainland China".

- The show is only published on the platforms listed under the group(s) you tick.
- A platform not listed here is not covered by your authorization.
- If we want to add a platform later, we will tell you first and publish only after you agree.
:::

**Version: v2026.10.03**　When you sign, the version in force on the day you sign is the one that applies. Every change to this page is kept in the project [repository](https://github.com/Limooooo-Studio/Limooo-Website) commit history, so older versions can always be looked up.

> Checked: mainland platforms on 2026-09-27, overseas platforms on 2026-10-03. Entities and policy URLs change; always confirm

### What is sent to overseas platforms

Under China's Personal Information Protection Law, before personal information is provided to an overseas recipient you must be told the points below. Ticking "Platforms outside mainland China" means you have read and agreed to them:

- **What is provided**: your likeness and voice, and the name, nickname or stage name you give us (as it appears in footage, subtitles, descriptions or credits). Your phone number and email are **never** provided to any platform.
- **Purpose**: publicly releasing *Fengxing Mizong* episodes, behind-the-scenes clips, trailers and promotional material.
- **Method**: we upload the finished video to our accounts on the platforms listed above, and each platform stores, displays, recommends and distributes it under its own rules. How a platform handles viewer data (views, likes, comments) is governed by that platform's privacy policy.
- **Your rights**: you can contact us at any time to have content involving you taken down, adjusted or deleted (contact details are in the confirmation form), or you can contact the platform directly through the channels listed below.
